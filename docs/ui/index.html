The desk kit

Every note on the Glasswood desk is built from these parts (js/ui/kit.js), with one set of sizes in css/glasswood.css: card padding --pad, one line --row, a head --head, a seal --seal, column width --col. New features should be made from these parts and placed in one of the slots below.

Slots

Both columns are made of named slots in a fixed order. Empty slots take no space. The time card keeps one height by day and by night, so the cards below it never jump.

villagename and gold; later other resources
viewsthe three tabs
goalthe current goal and its reward
handlater: your villager cards and supplies
toolssettings knob, bottom left
the globe
timethe night: preview by day, the battle by night
focuswhat the view is about: a place, the barricades, a building, a card
detaillater: a second card, such as the villager sent on an expedition

Cards

A card has three zones, each optional: a head (icon, title, one small thing on the right), content (facts, meters, lists), and a foot (an action, tokens or one hint).

Parts

The pieces that go inside a card.

Trading cards (planned)

Villagers and creatures will use the same card with a portrait in the content zone. The head carries the name and level.
